Frequently Asked Questions

How do I start a ritual?

Open CENTURION, choose a ritual category from the dashboard (breathwork, cold exposure, meditation, or movement), and tap the start button. Each ritual includes a precision timer and ambient audio cues.

The audio is not playing.

Ritual sound effects play in the foreground only. Make sure your device is not in silent mode and that the CENTURION app is open. The app does not play audio in the background.

How do I restore my subscription?

Go to Settings inside the app and tap "Restore Purchases". Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple ID used for the original purchase.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. Open Settings on your iPhone → tap your Apple ID at the top → Subscriptions → CENTURION → Cancel Subscription.

Will my ritual streak reset if I miss a day?

Streaks reset after 24 hours of inactivity. CENTURION sends a reminder before the streak window closes so you can preserve your discipline.

Does CENTURION require an account?

No. CENTURION works fully on-device. There is no login, no cloud sync, and no data is sent to external servers.

What iOS version is required?

CENTURION requires iOS 15.1 or later. The app is optimized for iPhone.
